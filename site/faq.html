Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to ask.

Eligibility, the exam, learning, careers and the fine print. Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us on +91 94499 04569.

What is K21 Series?

K21 Series is a centralized global education system and competitive exam created by Mahasaraswathi Education Foundation Pvt. Ltd. It combines a world-class curriculum, 600+ courses and a single exam held every six months.

Is the Foundation registered?

Yes. Mahasaraswathi Education Foundation Private Limited has completed its registration.

What's the minimum qualification?

A 7th standard pass, or the equivalent grade in your country.

How many seats are there?

1,000 admissions across 193 countries.

When does it start?

Opening shortly. Administration has started and the study materials are prepared. Book now to secure your place.

Can a 12- or 13-year-old really handle K21?

Yes. K21 starts at Foundation level and moves at your pace. The exam is tough, but you have a fresh attempt every six months to grow into it.

How often is the exam held?

Once every six months.

Why is it called one of the toughest exams?

Because it is modelled on the selection processes of top global companies, and it covers knowledge across twelve engineering disciplines, business and finance in one sitting.

What happens if I don't do well the first time?

Take it again in six months. Each sitting is a chance to move up the K21 scale, and your best result represents you.

What subjects are covered?

Engineering (600+ courses), business knowledge and administration across 193 countries, financial management, life skills, languages, and reasoning and IQ development.

Is K21 online or offline?

Both: global online classrooms and smart learning, plus practical labs.

What does "build your IQ power" mean?

Structured training in creative thinking, focus, memory and analytical thinking, the four abilities behind every strong learner and every K21 exam question.

Who writes the study materials?

K21's academic team, drawing on the strategies and lessons of global business leaders and industry experts. The materials are already prepared.

Is there financial management even for engineering learners?

Yes. Financial management is part of every K21 learner's education, because every engineer, founder and leader eventually manages money.

Does K21 guarantee a job?

K21 prepares you for the selection processes of global companies and helps connect learners with opportunities. Selection is always the employer's decision.
