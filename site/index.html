What is K21?

The gold standard in global learning.

K21 Series is a centralized global education system by the Mahasaraswathi Education Foundation: one world-class curriculum, one rigorous exam every six months, and one standard recognised in 193 countries.

01 · The Exam

One of the toughest competitive exams in the world.

The K21 exam tests what you can actually do with what you know, not what you've memorised. It's held every six months, and every attempt measures you against the same global benchmark.

Learn about the exam →

02 · The Knowledge Hub

600+ courses. One place.

Engineering, business, administration, finance and life skills, brought together in one of the strongest knowledge hubs in education. You don't have to pick between technical and business. You build both.

Explore learning →

03 · The Outcome

Selected the way the world's best companies select.

K21's assessment follows the methods high-end global companies use to hire. Preparing for K21 is preparing for the career you want.

See career outcomes →

04 · The K21 Difference

The depth of 12 engineering degrees. The focus of one exam.

A degree takes four years and covers one discipline. K21 builds knowledge across twelve engineering disciplines and tests it through a single, rigorous exam.

Our inspiration

The standard we've set ourselves.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya grew up in Muddenahalli, a village in Karnataka, and became one of the greatest engineers India has known. He built dams, banks, colleges and industries that still serve the state, and India celebrates Engineers' Day on his birthday, 15 September.

He believed that discipline, honesty and hard work, applied patiently, lift whole communities. That's the standard behind K21: an engineer's rigour, open to every learner, wherever they were born. Engineer, Dewan of Mysore and Bharat Ratna, he proved that a student from a village could build for a nation.
