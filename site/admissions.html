Admissions

Admissions open shortly. If you have passed 7th standard, you can book your seat now.

Passed 7th standard? You're eligible.

That, or the equivalent grade in your country, is all you need to take the K21 Series exam. There are no degree requirements. Students, graduates, working professionals and career changers are all welcome. What matters is your commitment to meeting the K21 standard.

Why only 1,000 seats?

Because a global standard has to be protected.

Limiting admissions means every learner gets personal attention, every seat is earned, and the K21 name keeps its value.
