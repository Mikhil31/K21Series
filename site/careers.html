The K21 career readiness programme

Practise every round before it counts.

  1. Aptitude rounds

    Timed practice in the format global companies use.

  2. Technical rounds

    Problem-solving across disciplines, not only your favourite subject.

  3. Case and group rounds

    Thinking and communicating as a team under pressure.

  4. Interviews

    Mock interviews with feedback on content, confidence and communication.

  5. Profile building

    Presenting your K21 result, projects and skills to employers.
