About the Foundation

One world. One system. One standard.

The story, the belief and the people behind K21 Series, a centralized global education system by Mahasaraswathi Education Foundation.

Who we are

Knowledge should belong to everyone willing to earn it.

K21 Series is created by Mahasaraswathi Education Foundation Private Limited, a registered education company.

The Foundation is named after Saraswathi, the goddess of knowledge, learning and the arts, and its purpose matches the name. A brilliant 13-year-old in a small town has the same potential as one in a global capital, but not the same access to curriculum, assessment or recognition. K21 exists to close that gap.

Our belief

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

We built K21 to close that gap with four commitments.

01

One World

Learners from 193 countries, in one community.

02

One System

A single, centralized curriculum and exam, so nobody is held back by their board or syllabus.

03

One Standard

The same bar for everyone. Your result means the same thing in every country.

04

One Future

A generation prepared together for the challenges no single country can solve alone.

Our motto

Three words we hold ourselves to.

Educate

World-class knowledge, broad and deep, across engineering, business, finance and life skills.

Empower

Turning that knowledge into skills, confidence and character you can use anywhere.

Excel

Holding every learner to one global standard, and helping them meet it.

Mission and vision

Why we exist.

Our mission

Open the same door for every learner.

To give every learner in the world, from the age of 12, access to one world-class curriculum, one fair and rigorous exam, and one standard of achievement that opens doors in every country.

Our vision

A future decided by ability, not birthplace.

A world where a learner's future depends on what they know and can do, not on where they were born, which board they studied under, or what their family could afford.

Why Saraswathi?

Knowledge, protected by a high standard.

In Indian tradition, Saraswathi is the goddess of knowledge, wisdom, music and the arts. She holds the veena for creativity and a book for knowledge, and her calm, focused expression stands for discipline.

Our emblem places her inside a shield, which stands for protecting knowledge and holding a standard, and surrounds her with a laurel wreath, the ancient sign of achievement. Together it says what K21 stands for: knowledge, protected by a high standard, rewarded with achievement.
